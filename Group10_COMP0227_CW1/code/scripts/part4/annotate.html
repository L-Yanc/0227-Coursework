Wall marker centres

Select a wall pair and endpoint, then click its precise centre. Click again to reposition it. After clicking the image, use arrow keys for 1 image pixel, or Shift + arrow for 5 pixels. The blue outline means keyboard adjustment is active.

Aim for 4–6 views per cross centre: 16–24 selections total across the four crosses (minimum 3 each, 12 total). You do not need every frame. Choose sharp, well-separated camera positions with translation, keeping the same physical cross intersection as the target. Skip hidden or cropped crosses.

Shortcuts: 5 = wall_50, 1 = wall_100; A / B = endpoint; S = save; Space = next frame.
